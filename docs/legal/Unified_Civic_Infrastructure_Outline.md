# **1\. One sentence**

Citizen Gardens is a Wyoming unincorporated nonprofit association that prototypes a multiplicative society; a licensed operator hosts Phase Mirror; Sovereignty Nodes do the work; surplus funds purpose; credentials attest practitioners; equity never mints a diploma.

# **2\. What this infrastructure is**

Unified civic infrastructure here means four jobs in one federation, not one company that does everything.

* Legal person that cannot be acquired for private residual claims.

* Work topology that scales by splitting teams, not by adding managers.

* Decision protocol that names dissonance and binds owners, metrics, and horizons.

* Duty code for people who hold power over neighbors, data, and treasuries.

* Value system that counts reciprocity as social energy and refuses to cash it out as a dividend.

The playground is Civic R\&D as a live Sovereignty Node. The engine is Phase Mirror plus Multiplicity. The housing is the UNA, DUNA-ready.

# **6\. Capital — four doors, no fifth**

People may fund purpose. They may not own the association. “We don’t want a big return” does not make membership an investment contract.

| Door | Instrument | They receive | They never receive |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Gift | Gift agreement to the UNA | Acknowledgment and the public node card | Equity, extra votes, certs |
| Sponsor | Sponsor-class membership | One member, one vote; updates | A dividend |
| Recoverable grant | Written note, 0% or below-market | Capital back on schedule, no upside | A slice of software |
| Operator subscription | LLC membership interest | Economics of the hosted oracle | UNA control, Examiner veto, automatic PMCP |

Greenhouse stake is a fifth rail only inside a node project: living dividend of produce, access, or service — not interest. Credits are not dollars. Cash-out stays capped and counsel-reviewed.

Operator remittance of a published share of surplus is how the shop pays the garden. Label the wire remittance.

# **7\. Dual seat — practice and oracle**

Lined-up counterparties who want to practice and to own the hosted oracle take two seats. Two papers. One firewall.

| Seat | Paper | How they get it |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| PMCP practitioner | PMCP agreement | Same four gates: mastery interview, vertical playbook, compliance clearance, supervised engagement |
| Oracle owner | Operator subscription / OA | Capital for membership interest in the managed-service company |

## **Firewall**

* Equity close does not issue PMCP. Automatic-cert-on-signing in the Director term sheet is a defect. Strike it.

* PMCP failure does not unwind equity. Revocation does not confiscate shares. Using the mark after revocation is a separate breach.

* Anyone at or above 10% of the operator, or holding a board seat, cannot sit as Examiner on any candidate, including themselves.

* Calibration data is not collateral and not an LLC asset to pledge.

* Operator board may fund Examiner / Guardian. It may not instruct a pass.

* Citizen Gardens keeps a written license to the hosted tools. Owners cannot pull playground access without notice and a grace period.

* First-cohort extras that survive: locked revenue-share rate on work they personally deliver; playbook co-authorship; advisory on methodology.

* First-cohort extras that die: automatic cert; no recertification review; exclusive lock on a vertical purchased with equity.

Yield lives only on the shop side: seats, packs, exam fees, and revenue share on delivered branded work. A certificate does not mint. A certificate attests.

# **8\. Surplus pointed at purpose**

Surplus is what remains after reasonable compensation and named reserves. Members have no residual claim on it. Paychecks, tools, equipment, and sites are costs when they pass four tests: purpose, reasonableness, title in the association, written authority.

| Item | Allowed as | Fails when |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Staff | Wages for work, W-2 or 1099, role card | Profit share, bonus tied to leftover cash, payee approving their own deal |
| Tools / equipment | Kit-of-parts; association title; node-card line | Personal take-home; founder sale above a stranger’s price |
| Satellite site | Node or named back-office with public hours | Residence subsidy; prestige address with no metric card |

# **9\. Who decides a role or a purchase**

| Decision | Who says yes | Funding | Cap |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Role inside an existing node | Node by consent; admin L0 check only | That node’s envelope | Envelope must cover 90 days |
| New department / new place | Members under §7; named sponsor \+ 90-day metric | New envelope first | No hire before the envelope |
| Commons role (records, treasury, ICT) | Admins propose; assembly confirms if ongoing | Commons line | Overhead target ≤15% |
| Equipment ≤ $2,500 in envelope | Node | Node envelope | Title to Citizen Gardens |
| Any movement \> $2,500 | Treasurer \+ one other admin | Named envelope or named grant | Dual control |
| Material asset | Members, majority after 14-day notice | Commons or restricted gift | Vehicle, lease \>12 mo, land, or ≥ $5,000 proposed |
| Related-party deal | Same as above \+ non-payee countersignature | Same | Payee does not approve |

Until first assembly, signers of the principles are administrators. Their written authority: EIN, agent, register, counsel, contracts under $2,500.

Three-way test pauses a node after two failed quarters: member dignity, community outcome, solvency. Pause is a kill-switch, not a punishment.

Recommended material-asset floor to bind in v1.2: $5,000, any vehicle, any lease longer than 12 months, any interest in land.

# **11\. L0 invariants**

* No member profit distribution.

* No node cameras in a declared privacy zone.

* No membership list used off-purpose.

* No administrator binds the association beyond written authority.

* No third party offers Phase Mirror as a managed service without a commercial license.

* Youth membership requires confirmed guardian consent.

* Consent is withdrawable. Withdrawal is logged, not argued.

* Credits do not buy votes. Tokens do not overweight votes.

* The association is not a PAC.
